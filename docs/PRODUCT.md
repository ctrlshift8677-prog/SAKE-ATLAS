# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS generated by a small Python build script (no npm, no database). Owner previews with `python -m http.server` and uploads a folder to static hosting; every sake gets its own URL for search and sharing.

## Users

Two audiences, confirmed by the owner as equally important:
- Sake-curious Taiwanese readers who don't yet know what to order or buy; they need plain-language guidance (what it tastes like, where to drink it) more than spec sheets.
- Enthusiasts who already know brands and rice varieties; they come to compare, browse by prefecture/rice/brewery, and read honest first-hand notes.

## Product Purpose

A personal sake tasting archive (SAKE ATLAS) turned into a public site the owner can eventually monetize (tasting events, shop partnerships, brewery-travel guides). Success = visitors stay, explore several bottles, and come back.

## Positioning

First-hand records of 203 bottles actually tasted, mostly in Taipei sake bars (NIHONSHU Sake Space, etc.) and Osaka, organized on a map of Japan. Honest notes including "would not buy again".

## Operating Context

Read on phones (LINE/IG shares) and desktop. Visitors often arrive on a single bottle page from search.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Data: data/data.json (203 products; brand, name, brewery, prefecture, type, rice, polish ratio, tastings with date/place/shop/method/volume, sources). Owner says full tasting data exists offline and will be added later; flavor fields are optional and must degrade gracefully.
- Taiwan law (菸酒管理法): no online alcohol sales or ordering; alcohol promotion must not encourage drinking; warnings 「禁止酒駕」「飲酒過量，有害健康」 required on promotional content. Site sells nothing.
- Prices are not public yet.

## Brand Commitments

- Name SAKE ATLAS, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan).
- Owner chose to KEEP the ink-wash (水墨) look and the clickable Japan prefecture map; upgrade on that basis. Assets: static/map-backdrop.webp (watercolor mountains + waves with gold leaf), prefecture outlines, region color set.

## Evidence on Hand

- 9 bottle photos (static/images, 3:4, bar counter, bottle + glass).
- 1 full tasting note (S0031), 5 short product notes, 5 would-buy-again flags.
- Do not fabricate tasting notes, scores, flavor values or prices.

## Product Principles

1. Help people choose, not drink more.
2. First-hand truth over spec sheets; empty fields stay honest.
3. Every bottle is a destination page that leads to another bottle.
4. The map is the signature; keep it central.
